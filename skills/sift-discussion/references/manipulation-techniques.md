# Common manipulation techniques in political messages

- **Cherry-picking** – selecting only data that fits the conclusion.
- **Missing context** – real quote/image/number used in a different time, place or meaning.
- **Misleading statistics** – absolute vs. relative numbers, missing denominator, truncated axes, correlation presented as causation.
- **Straw man** – distorting the opponent's position to attack it.
- **Ad hominem / whataboutism** – attacking the person or deflecting instead of addressing the claim.
- **False dilemma** – presenting two options when more exist.
- **Appeal to emotion / fear / outrage** – emotional framing replacing evidence.
- **Loaded language** – words that carry a conclusion.
- **Fake or unnamed authority** – "experts say", "studies show" without a reference.
- **Slippery slope** – unsupported chain of consequences.
- **Doctored or recycled media** – edited, AI-generated or old images/videos.
- **Satire taken literally** – parody sites presented as news.
- **Bandwagon / false consensus** – "everyone knows".
